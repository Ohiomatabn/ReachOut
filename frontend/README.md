<h1>About BridgeTech</h1>
<b>BridgeTech</b> is a civic tech web application designed to bridge the communication gap between citizens and government stakeholders, starting locally within Kogi State. The platform serves as a direct, transparent conduit for everyday people to report societal damages, infrastructure failures, and community needs, while providing public officials with the data-driven insights required to prioritize and execute solutions.

<h2>The Problem</h2>
In many local communities, a massive disconnect exists between the populace and local government authorities. When critical infrastructure fails—such as roads developing severe potholes, water pipes bursting, or public facilities breaking down citizens lack a reliable, centralized, and accountable channel to report these issues. Traditional methods rely on noisy social media threads or slow physical petitions, leaving grievances unaddressed and public officials in the dark regarding grassroots needs.

<h2>The Solution: BridgeTech</h2>
BridgeTech eliminates this friction by computerization and streamlining civic reporting. It transforms vague complaints into structured, trackable data tickets that ensure accountability on both sides.

<h2>Value Proposition</h2>
<b>For Citizens:</b> A voice that reaches decision-makers directly, ensuring local problems are seen and addressed without administrative runaround.

<b>For Government:</b> Real-time data and actionable insights from the ground up, allowing ministries to allocate resources efficiently where they are needed most.
